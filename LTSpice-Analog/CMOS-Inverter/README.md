# CMOS Inverter — DC Transfer Characteristics
**Tool:** LTSpice XVII
**Topic:** Analog CMOS Design

## What I Built
Simulated a CMOS inverter and analysed its
DC transfer characteristics and switching threshold.

## Schematic
![Schematic](schematic_screenshot.png)

## Simulation Result

## Key Result
- Switching threshold: ~1.2V (for VDD = 2.5V)
- Output swings rail to rail

## What I Learned
How PMOS and NMOS work together and how
W/L ratio affects the switching point.
